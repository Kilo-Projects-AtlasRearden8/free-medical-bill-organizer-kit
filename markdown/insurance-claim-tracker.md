## Before you submit a claim

- [ ] Service is covered under my plan?
- [ ] Provider is in-network (or I understand out-of-network costs)?
- [ ] Prior authorization obtained, if required?
- [ ] Claim form complete and signed?
- [ ] Itemized bill attached?
- [ ] Copy kept for my records?
- [ ] Submission date and method noted?
- [ ] Follow-up reminder set (usually 30–45 days)?
