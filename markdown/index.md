# Markdown Forms — Free Medical Bill Organizer Kit

Plain-text, printable versions of every form in the kit. Print any page and fill it in by hand, or copy into your favorite notes app. No account needed. Nothing is stored online.

**Credit:** Asdale Dynamics, LLC  
**Notice:** Education only — not billing advice. Confirm all charges, payments, and deadlines with your provider and insurer.

## Forms

| Form | What it tracks | Markdown |
|---|---|---|
| Medical Bill Tracker | Bills, amounts, insurance payments, due dates, payment status | [medical-bill-tracker.md](medical-bill-tracker.md) |
| Insurance Claim Tracker | Claims, claim numbers, billed/allowed/paid, denials, appeals | [insurance-claim-tracker.md](insurance-claim-tracker.md) |
| EOB Review Worksheet | Explanation-of-benefits line by line: billed vs. allowed vs. paid | [eob-review-worksheet.md](eob-review-worksheet.md) |
| Payment Plan Tracker | Payment plan terms, monthly payments, due dates, payment log | [payment-plan-tracker.md](payment-plan-tracker.md) |
| Charity Care Application Tracker | Charity care applications, documents, decisions, follow-ups | [charity-care-application-tracker.md](charity-care-application-tracker.md) |

## How to use

1. Click a form name above to open its Markdown page.
2. Print the page from your browser, or copy the text into a notes app and print from there.
3. Fill in the blanks by hand. Keep the pages with your medical paperwork.
4. Nothing is saved by this kit. No account, no tracking, nothing leaves your device.

Return to the [kit hub](../forms/index.html) for the fillable HTML versions.
