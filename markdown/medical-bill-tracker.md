## Follow-up checklist

- [ ] Bill matches EOB amounts?
- [ ] Insurance paid what it said it would?
- [ ] Due dates marked on calendar?
- [ ] Payment confirmations saved?
- [ ] Errors noted and disputed?
- [ ] Payment plan set up where needed?
- [ ] Charity care considered for large bills?
- [ ] Files saved (paper or digital)?
