## EOB review checklist

- [ ] Patient name and ID are correct?
- [ ] Dates of service match my records?
- [ ] No duplicate charges for the same service?
- [ ] Procedure codes match what I received?
- [ ] Billed amount matches the provider's bill?
- [ ] Allowed amount matches my plan's contracted rate?
- [ ] Deductible, copay, and coinsurance applied correctly?
- [ ] Insurance paid its stated share?
- [ ] "You pay" matches what the provider billed me?
