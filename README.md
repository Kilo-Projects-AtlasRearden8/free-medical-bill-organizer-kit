# Free Medical Bill Organizer Kit

Fillable, printable worksheets for tracking medical bills, insurance claims, explanation-of-benefits (EOBs), payment plans, and charity-care applications. No account needed. Nothing is stored online. Education only — not billing advice.

**Credit:** Asdale Dynamics, LLC

## What's inside

| Form | What it tracks |
|---|---|
| [Medical Bill Tracker](forms/medical-bill-tracker.html) | Bills received, amounts, insurance payments, due dates, payment status |
| [Insurance Claim Tracker](forms/insurance-claim-tracker.html) | Claims submitted, claim numbers, amounts billed/allowed/paid, denials, appeals |
| [EOB Review Worksheet](forms/eob-review-worksheet.html) | Explanation-of-benefits line by line: billed vs. allowed vs. paid vs. what you owe |
| [Payment Plan Tracker](forms/payment-plan-tracker.html) | Payment plan terms, monthly payments, due dates, payment log |
| [Charity Care Application Tracker](forms/charity-care-application-tracker.html) | Charity care / financial-assistance applications, documents, decisions, follow-ups |

Markdown versions of every form live in [`markdown/`](markdown/) for printing, copying into notes apps, or adapting to your needs.

## How to use

1. Open any form in `forms/` in your browser (for example `forms/index.html` for the full kit).
2. Type into the fields directly, or print the page and fill it in by hand. Each form has a **Print this form** button; print dialogs hide the buttons automatically.
3. Keep the printed pages with your medical paperwork, or save a PDF from your browser's print dialog.
4. Nothing is saved by this kit. No account, no tracking, nothing leaves your device unless you print, save, or share the page yourself.
