# Free Medical Bill Organizer Kit

Fillable, printable worksheets for tracking medical bills, insurance claims, explanation-of-benefits (EOBs), payment plans, and charity-care applications. No account needed. Nothing is stored online. Education only — not billing advice.

## What's inside

| Form | What it tracks |
|---|---|
| [Medical Bill Tracker](forms/medical-bill-tracker.html) | Every bill received: provider, dates, amounts, insurance payments, due dates, payment status |
| [Insurance Claim Tracker](forms/insurance-claim-tracker.html) | Claims submitted: claim numbers, amounts billed/allowed/paid, denials, and appeals |
| [EOB Review Worksheet](forms/eob-review-worksheet.html) | Explanation-of-benefits line by line: billed vs. allowed vs. paid vs. what you owe |
| [Payment Plan Tracker](forms/payment-plan-tracker.html) | Payment plan terms, monthly payments, due dates, and a log of every payment |
| [Charity Care Application Tracker](forms/charity-care-application-tracker.html) | Charity care / financial-assistance applications, documents, decisions, follow-ups |

Each form is also available as plain-text Markdown in [`markdown/`](markdown/) — print it, copy it into a notes app, or adapt it to your needs.

## How to use it

You don't need to install anything or create an account. Just open a form in your web browser and fill it in.

1. **Open the kit.** Open [`forms/index.html`](forms/index.html) in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox — any modern browser works). You'll see all five forms as cards.
2. **Pick a form.** Click the form you need — for example, "Medical Bill Tracker."
3. **Fill it in.** Type directly into the fields on screen, or print the page and fill it in by hand. Every form has a **"Print this form"** button. When you print, the buttons and navigation disappear automatically, leaving just the clean worksheet.
4. **Save or print.** Click **Print this form**, or use your browser's print dialog (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). Choose "Save as PDF" if you'd like a digital copy. Printed pages go well with your medical paperwork.
5. **Start over anytime.** Nothing is saved online. Refresh the page and the fields clear — nothing leaves your device unless you print, save, or share it yourself.
